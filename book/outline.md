# The Profit Tree

*A working outline for a higher-education book on strategic profitability*

## Concept and framing

This book is aimed at higher-education students and practitioners, written in a popular-science register rather than as a conventional Norwegian "fagbok": rigorous enough to teach the underlying technical tools, but written to be read, not just referenced. Throughout, profitability is framed through a single extended metaphor — **the Profit Tree** — with four parts that map onto four analytical modules:

| Tree part | Module | Analytical lens |
|---|---|---|
| Trunk | 1 | Strategy analysis |
| Branches | 2 | Operational analysis (costing) |
| Leaves & Fruit | 3 | Revenue analysis (pricing) |
| Roots | 4 | Sustainability & ethics (social license to operate) |

Each module blends technical method (frameworks, models, calculations) with strategic and managerial judgment — the "how to compute it" and the "how to decide with it."
