# Chapter 15: Organization of Humans — and Robots

**Part:** Part 4 — The Root System
**Tree element:** The roots — now shared with artificial systems.

## Premise

More and more of business decision-making is now shared with robots and AI. This chapter is about how to delegate tasks well, what role AI should play in different types of decisions, and how to align that delegation with strategy and value proposition.

## Content to cover

- Extend Chapter 14's "who makes the decision, and how well" question to include AI/automated systems as decision-makers or decision-support alongside humans.
- Present a framework — the **AI compass** — for navigating implementation choices: which decisions to automate, which to keep human, and which to structure as human-AI collaboration, and on what basis that choice should be made.
- Explicitly tie the delegation choice back to strategy and value proposition (Part 1) — the right human/AI split is not a generic best practice but depends on what the firm is trying to be and offer.
- Keep the treatment applied and framework-driven rather than a general AI-hype or AI-ethics essay — the chapter's job is decision-rights allocation, in service of the book's profitability argument.

## Writing guidelines (for the author)

- Since the "AI compass" is a named proprietary framework, give it a clear, teachable structure early in the chapter (the axes/dimensions it uses) before applying it — readers should be able to redraw it from memory.
- Ground the framework in 2–3 concrete decision types (e.g., pricing decisions, customer-service triage, capacity/scheduling decisions) run through the compass, rather than treating it abstractly.
- Explicitly avoid a generic "future of AI in business" narrative — keep the chapter's scope to decision delegation and its strategic alignment, consistent with the book's applied, decision-focused register throughout.
- Close by connecting back to strategy (Part 1) explicitly, since that's the anchor that keeps this chapter from reading as a bolt-on tech chapter.
