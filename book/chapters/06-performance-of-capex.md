# Chapter 6: Performance of CAPEX

**Part:** Part 2 — Pruning the Branches
**Tree element:** The branches — their long-term structural investment.

## Premise

Where Chapter 5 dealt with operating expenses, this chapter deals with capital expenditure: how capital costs are allocated over time, and how that allocation differs across business models.

## Content to cover

- Principles of capital cost allocation over time (depreciation logic, cost of capital, and how CAPEX timing distorts short-run profitability measures if not handled carefully).
- Comparing business models that place CAPEX differently: e.g., "product as a service" (where CAPEX sits with the provider and is most relevant to their economics) vs. selling a product outright (where CAPEX is transferred to the buyer).
- How to analyze profitability performance under each model on a comparable basis — the core analytical challenge readers should walk away able to handle.

## Key frameworks & references

- Capital cost allocation / depreciation and cost-of-capital logic
- Product-as-a-service vs. product-sale business model comparison

## Connections

- **Builds on:** Chapter 4's cost-causality principle, applied here to capital rather than operating costs; structurally parallel to Chapter 5.
- **Sets up:** Chapter 7 (Investments), which explicitly combines OPEX and CAPEX perspectives.

## Writing guidelines (for the author)

- Keep the depreciation/cost-of-capital mechanics at the level needed to support the business-model comparison — this is not a corporate-finance textbook chapter; the payoff is the "as-a-service vs. sell" comparison, not the mechanics for their own sake.
- Use one running example business (e.g., industrial equipment, vehicles, or software/hardware) analyzed under both a product-as-a-service and a product-sale model, so the comparison is concrete rather than abstract.
- Make explicit why comparing profitability across these two models is *not* apples-to-apples without adjusting for how/when CAPEX is recognized — this is the chapter's central insight.
- Keep this chapter roughly parallel in structure and length to Chapter 5 so the OPEX/CAPEX pairing reads as a deliberate diptych.
