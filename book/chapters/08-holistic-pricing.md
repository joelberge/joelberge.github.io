# Chapter 8: Holistic Pricing

**Part:** Part 3 — The Leaves and the Berries
**Tree element:** The berries — what the customer actually meets.

## Premise

Pricing is more than a number; it is the spearhead of strategy. It is what customers meet directly, so alignment between the value proposition and pricing is crucial. Pricing is therefore an inherent strategic decision — but there are also distinct methods for determining it, each requiring a different intensity of information.

## Content to cover

- Reframe pricing as a strategic act, not an administrative afterthought — link explicitly back to Part 1's value-proposition/positioning discussion (Chapter 2).
- Present the three broad pricing methods as a typology, not a hierarchy:
  - **Cost-based pricing**
  - **Market-based pricing**
  - **Customer-based pricing**
- Make the key point explicit: these methods are not competing alternatives to pick "the right one" from — they require different levels of information intensity, and mature pricing practice often blends them.
- Set up the rest of Part 3: Chapter 9 goes deep on customer-based pricing's information requirements, Chapter 10 on measuring realized customer profitability, Chapter 11 on setting today's price levels analytically.

## Key frameworks & references

- Cost-based, market-based, and customer-based pricing typology
- Value proposition–pricing alignment (link back to Ch. 2's DNA-of-the-tree discussion)

## Writing guidelines (for the author)

- Open with a vivid case where pricing clearly *was* the strategy (or clearly betrayed it) to make "pricing as spearhead of strategy" land before any typology.
- Present the three pricing methods evenhandedly as a typology differentiated mainly by information intensity — avoid implying customer-based pricing is simply "the best" one; each has a legitimate use case depending on what information the firm can realistically gather.
- This chapter should feel like Part 3's own version of Chapter 1 (an orienting, conceptual chapter before the technical ones) — keep it light on calculation, heavy on framing.
- Explicitly flag for the reader that Chapters 9–11 will each pick up one thread from this chapter, so Part 3's internal structure is visible early.
